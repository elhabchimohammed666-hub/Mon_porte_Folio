Who Am I?

I'm Elhabchi Mohammed, a passionate Full-Stack Developer based in Morocco 🇲🇦 with a deep commitment to building robust, scalable web applications that solve real-world problems. My journey in software development began with a fascination for how logical structures can create elegant user experiences.

On the backend, I thrive in designing clean RESTful APIs, architecting relational database schemas, and enforcing security best practices (OWASP standards, input validation, and authentication flows). I believe that solid architecture is the invisible foundation of every great product.

On the frontend, I treat every interface as a conversation between the product and its user. I care deeply about responsiveness, accessibility, and the subtle micro-interactions that make an application feel truly polished and professional.

Beyond code, I have a strong interest in cybersecurity — network analysis, vulnerability scanning — which gives me a unique security-first mindset when developing applications.
